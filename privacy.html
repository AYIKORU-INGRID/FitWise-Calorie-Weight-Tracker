2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and is not readable by us.
  • Profile and goals: your age, sex (used only for the calorie estimate), height, weight, activity level, goal weight and daily calorie target.
  • Tracker entries: the foods you log with their calories, your water glasses and your weight history.
  • Plan and usage: whether you have Premium and how many photo scans you used today.
  • Photos you choose to scan: sent to an AI provider for analysis. We do not store them.
  • Technical data: basic server and security logs such as IP address and time of requests, kept by our hosting and database providers.

3. Health-related information and your consent

Your weight, food log and goals relate to your health. Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act this is treated as special personal data. We use it only to run the app for you, and only with your consent, which you give when you create your account. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 8).

4. How we use your information

  • To create your account, keep you signed in and save your tracker.
  • To work out your calorie target and show your progress, tips and insights.
  • To limit photo scans per day and to manage Premium.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising. The in-app coach and tips are calculated on your device from your own entries and do not send your data to an AI service.
